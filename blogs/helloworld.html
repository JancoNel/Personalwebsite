Hello, Blog

Posted: 2025-07-01

So originally I never planned to make a blog.

This site was supposed to be a portfolio — you know, a minimal, sleek little page showing off the stuff I build when Im not being consumed by school, chess prep, or late-night code experiments.

But then this kid at school, chaotic energy and undiagnosed ADHD flying everywhere, takes one glance at my homepage and goes:

“Ah, looks like a blog site or something.”

And Ill be honest, I died a little inside. But then I realized... maybe he was onto something?

So here we are. Welcome to the blog I never meant to have. Ill post dev logs, random thoughts, maybe some breakdowns of stuff Im working on. No guarantees, but if youre reading this, congrats ig.

Peace,
